Welcome to this little space where we explore Earth and the Solar System together. I'm Tria Liza, a student in the International Program on Science Education at Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia.

I love turning science into something that feels simple, visual, and fun to learn. This website was created for curious junior high school students who want to understand how our planet works and how we fit into the vast universe around us.
