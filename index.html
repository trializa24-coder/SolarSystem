Learning Objectives

By the End of This Journey, You Will Be Able To…

01 · Remember
🪐

Name and sequence the planets of the Solar System

List the eight planets in order from the Sun and their main characteristics.

02 · Understand
🌍

Explain why we have day, night, and seasons on Earth

Explain how Earth's rotation and revolution cause day, night, and seasons.

03 · Understand
🌕

Describe how and why the Moon changes its appearance

Explain the Moon's phases using the positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon.

04 · Analyze
🌒

Distinguish between a solar eclipse and a lunar eclipse

Compare solar eclipses and lunar eclipses based on their causes and positions.

05 · Apply
🌡️

Relate the Sun's height in the sky to changes in temperature

Analyze how the Sun's position in the sky affects temperature.

06 · Create
🛠️

Build a model that shows an astronomical phenomenon

Create a model of an astronomical phenomenon and explain how it works.

🔭
