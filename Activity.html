🌍 Game 8 · SDG Challenge

SDG Challenge

The United Nations has 17 goals to make the world better by 2030. Can you connect space science to these real-world goals?

🌐 The SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals) are 17 goals set by the United Nations to make the world better for everyone by 2030. In this game, you'll discover how topics like the Sun, Moon, Earth, and Seasons connect to goals like Climate Action, Clean Water, and Life on Land. Science isn't just in the classroom — it shapes the future of our planet!
