🌍 Game 6 · SDG Challenge

SDG Challenge

Test your understanding of how space science connects to the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Can you match the science to the global impact?

🌐 The SDGs are 17 goals set by the United Nations to make the world better for everyone by 2030. In this game, you will discover how topics like the Sun, Moon, Earth, and Seasons are connected to goals like Climate Action, Clean Water, and Life on Land. Science isn't just in the classroom — it shapes the future of our planet!
